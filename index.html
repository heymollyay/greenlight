Why GreenLight

You're already driving there. Bring a friend.

GreenLight matches W&M students who have a car and a plan with students who need a lift going the same way. Drivers post the trips they're already taking. Riders grab an open seat and chip in for gas. No detours, no surge pricing, no awkward group-chat begging.

  • Drivers cover gas

    Every seat you fill puts money back in your tank for a trip you were taking anyway.

  • Riders save big

    Split the real cost of gas instead of paying ride-hail prices to the airport or back home.

  • Tribe only

    Every driver and rider signs up with a verified @wm.edu email, so you're riding with classmates.

How it works

Three steps to the road

  1. 01

    Browse the board

    Filter by grocery runs, airport trips, hometowns or campus commutes, and see exactly when each car leaves and where it picks up.

  2. 02

    Request a seat

    Pick a trip, send a quick note, and the driver confirms. You'll see the chip-in amount before you commit, so there are no surprises.

  3. 03

    Ride & chip in

    Meet at the pickup spot, enjoy the ride, and cover your share of the gas. Rate the trip so the community stays great.

  3. 03

    Riders chip in toward fuel for the trip you were already taking. Fuller car, emptier tank bill, smaller footprint.

    FAQ

    Good questions

    Is GreenLight a taxi or ride-hail service?

    Nope. Drivers only post trips they were already planning to take. Riders chip in to share the cost of gas. It's carpooling between classmates, not a for-hire service.

    Who can use GreenLight?

    Current William & Mary students with a valid @wm.edu email. Drivers also confirm they have a valid license and insurance before posting rides.

    How much does a ride cost?

    Drivers set a per-seat chip-in using our guide: about $10 per hour of driving. A campus commute or grocery run is $5, Richmond Airport is about $10, and a two-hour drive home is about $20. You'll always see the price before you request a seat.

    How do riders pay drivers?

    During the beta, riders pay drivers directly (for example, Venmo or cash) once the trip is done. In-app payments are on our roadmap.

    What if my plans change?

    Cancel your request or your ride as early as you can so others can grab the seat. Repeated last-minute cancellations will show up on your profile.

    Which airports do you cover?

    Wherever drivers are headed! The most common are Richmond (RIC), Newport News/Williamsburg (PHF) and Norfolk (ORF), plus D.C.-area airports around breaks.

    About us

    Made by students, for students

    GreenLight started with a simple observation on William & Mary's campus: every weekend, half-empty cars head to the same grocery stores, airports and hometowns while other students scramble for a ride. We're building the easiest way for the Tribe to fill those seats, save money and get where they're going together.
